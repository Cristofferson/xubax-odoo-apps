Self-Order Kiosk Quotation - WhatsApp

Free add-on: the customer gets the kiosk quotation on their WhatsApp.

What it adds

  • A Send it to my WhatsApp button in the kiosk cart of Self-Order Kiosk - Quotation.
  • The customer receives the quotation ticket as the image of your approved WhatsApp quotation template: the same one your POS uses, no new template to approve.
  • The message is kept in the quotation's chatter.
  • If WhatsApp fails, the quotation is saved anyway and the customer is told to ask at the counter.

Requirements

Odoo 19 Enterprise with WhatsApp · Self-Order Kiosk - Quotation · Sales, Quotations & Layaway from POS - WhatsApp (XUBAX). Installs by itself when both are present.
