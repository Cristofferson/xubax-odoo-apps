Showroom Wall — Xibo

Publish the showroom wall on a Xibo screen in one click, with no manual layout work.

One click

Pick the branch (point of sale) and its Xibo screen on the showroom screen and press Publish on the videowall. The layout is built on the canvas of that screen: a 5760×1080 videowall gets the whole surface.

Two ways to show it

  • Always on screen: the wall becomes the screen's default layout. The layout it replaces is remembered and Remove from the videowall puts it back.
  • Only while pieces are shown: the screen keeps its own layout and the wall takes over when a sales person sends a piece. A purchase thank-you always wins.

Free add-on. Requires Showroom Wall and the XUBAX Xibo connector (xibo_connector_pos).
