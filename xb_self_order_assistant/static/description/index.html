Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller (AI)

A seller with a face, a name and a voice on your kiosk. She greets whoever walks up, talks and listens in the customer's language, recommends products from your catalog with their real prices, puts them in the cart, and follows the customer to the videowall and to their phone.

Kiosk → videowall → phone

One conversation that travels with the customer. Everything she says and shows lands in Odoo.

She starts the conversation

Someone stops in front of the kiosk: she waves and says hello, out loud or in a bubble.

Full screen, with her photo and her voice. Hands-free: the customer just talks.

  • Eleven realistic avatars included (women and men), or upload your own photo.
  • Her name and voice: ten natural voices, with the accent and tone you describe ("warm, Mexican Spanish, unhurried").
  • Talks, listens or both. Typing always works too.
  • Any language: she answers in the language the customer uses.
  • Notices people with the tablet's camera (nothing is recorded), or with our Analitix camera app.
  • Reads barcodes and NFC cards with a USB/Bluetooth reader or the tablet's NFC: the customer shows a tag and she talks about that product.

Real products, real prices, a real sale

  • She searches the kiosk's own catalog and puts her picks at the top of the product list ("Lucía · selected for you").
  • Prices come from Odoo: the kiosk's pricelist, the price of the exact combination of options, never invented.
  • She adds the product to the cart with the options the customer chose and takes them to pay.
  • She knows your shop: delivery, payment methods, warranty, opening hours — you write it once.
  • Way of selling: consultative, balanced or a sales-driven floor seller who shows, recommends and closes.
  • Short answers, said out loud; the rest when the customer asks.

Goes with the customer

To the videowall (with the free Showroom Wall add-on): "look at the big screen!" — the product appears in large, and she goes with it, small, with subtitles and her voice on the wall speakers.

To the phone: a QR on the kiosk, or a tap on an NFC sticker.

The same conversation, with its history and products; the kiosk frees itself for the next customer.

WhatsApp, appointments, quotations, a person

  • "Send it to my WhatsApp": the customer types their number on a keypad and receives a page with the products and the conversation (free WhatsApp add-on, Odoo Enterprise). The contact and a CRM opportunity are created.
  • Appointments: she offers free times and books the visit in your calendar (free add-on for Odoo Appointments).
  • Printed quotation of the cart at the kiosk, or by WhatsApp (free add-on for our Self-Order Kiosk Quotation).
  • Call a person: a chat in Odoo, an alert on the cash register screens and/or an e-mail, to the people you choose, with a summary of what the customer wants.

Everything set up per kiosk, in one screen

Point of Sale › Settings › Mobile self-order & Kiosk › Virtual seller (AI): name, woman or man, avatar, voice, greeting, what she knows, how she sells, how she calls a person, the NFC link, the weekly summary and a daily spending limit. Each kiosk can have a different seller.

Know what your customers ask for

Point of Sale › Reporting › Virtual seller: every conversation, the products she showed, what went into the cart, appointments, WhatsApp, phone and wall, the sale it ended in and its cost.

What customers looked for — in red, what they asked for and you don't have.

A weekly e-mail with the numbers, the most shown products and what was asked for and not found.

Your keys, your costs, under control

  • She thinks with Claude (Anthropic) and listens and speaks with OpenAI. You use your own API keys: no subscription to us, you pay the AI providers directly.
  • A conversation usually costs a few US cents (3 to 6 cents in our shops). Every conversation shows its cost.
  • Daily limit per kiosk: when it is reached, the button hides until the next day.
  • Two ways to talk: step by step (listens, thinks, answers) or instant conversation (live voice, interruptible).
  • Voices are cached: the same sentence is never paid twice.

Free add-ons (install by themselves)

  • Virtual Seller + Showroom Wall — she takes products to the videowall and goes with them.
  • Virtual Seller + WhatsApp — "send it to my WhatsApp" (Odoo Enterprise).
  • Virtual Seller + Appointments — books visits (Odoo Enterprise).
  • Virtual Seller + Kiosk Quotation — printed or WhatsApp quotation of the cart.
  • Virtual Seller + Analitix — Analitix's camera tells her when someone stops in front of the kiosk.

Requirements

Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise · Point of Sale with Self-Order Kiosk · an Anthropic API key and an OpenAI API key · Python packages anthropic and requests · the kiosk opened over HTTPS (for the microphone and camera).
