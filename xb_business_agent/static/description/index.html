WhatsApp AI Agent

Your customers write to your WhatsApp at any hour. An AI agent answers them with your prices and hours, logs the lead in the CRM and books the appointment.

The problem

Most questions on WhatsApp are the same ones: how much is it, when are you open, do you have time on Tuesday? If nobody answers within minutes, the customer writes to the next business on the list.

Answering them takes your team away from the work, and at night or on weekends nobody answers at all.

What the agent does

The agent (Anthropic Claude) answers every message on your WhatsApp number, inside Odoo, in the language the customer writes in. It uses only what you wrote in its knowledge base, so it never makes up prices or promotions.

When someone is interested it creates the opportunity in your CRM, and when they want to book it offers real free slots and books the appointment in Odoo Appointments.

Everything stays in standard Odoo

  • Knowledge base (WhatsApp ▸ AI Agent): services, prices, hours and location, FAQs and rules. Edit it any time; the next answer already uses it.
  • CRM: native opportunities with medium WhatsApp, assigned to your team in turns, with what the customer said.
  • Appointments: the agent books only the appointment types you choose, with their own hours, staff and duration. It can also show or cancel the customer's upcoming appointments.
  • Reads what customers send: photos, PDFs (receipts, forms, documents) and voice notes (transcribed with OpenAI, optional).
  • Hands over to a person when it doesn't know, for payments or complaints, sensitive matters, or when the customer asks. Your team gets an activity and a message in their inbox.
  • Log with costs: every reply, the tools it used and its cost in USD.

You stay in control

  • Rehearsal mode is on by default: the agent writes its answers as internal notes in the chat, without sending them, until you are happy.
  • Test numbers: limit the agent to a few numbers while you try it.
  • If someone from your team replies in the chat, the agent goes quiet in that conversation. Type #agente or #bot to hand it back.
  • The agent only sees and cancels the appointments of the person it is talking to.

Setup in 5 minutes

  1. Connect your number in WhatsApp ▸ Configuration ▸ WhatsApp Business Accounts (standard Odoo).
  2. Open the account and, in AI Agent, paste your Anthropic API key and choose who answers and which appointments it can book.
  3. Write your knowledge base.
  4. Turn the agent on with rehearsal mode, read its notes, and turn rehearsal off.

Requirements and costs

  • Odoo 19 Enterprise with WhatsApp, CRM and Appointments.
  • An Anthropic API key (console.anthropic.com). You pay Anthropic for what you use: with Claude Sonnet a typical answer costs about 1 to 2 US cents. Choose Opus for the best answers or Haiku for the lowest cost.
  • Optional: an OpenAI API key to transcribe voice notes.
  • The Python library anthropic on the server (pip install anthropic).
  • Interface in English and Spanish.
