1. Architecture: what runs where

Vision runs in the store. Odoo only ever receives JSON.

Camera ──► mini-PC in the store (edge agent)
              │  detects, tracks, crosses the line, matches faces locally
              │  discards the frame in memory
              ▼
          HTTPS  POST /analitix/api/v1/events
              ▼
            Odoo (this addon)  ──► counts, visits, conversion, alerts

The addon has no computer-vision dependency — no torch, no insightface, no opencv. That lives in the agent, which ships separately with its own installer. A third party can write their own agent against doc/API.md without reading this addon's source.

2. Hardware by camera role

PositionCameraComputeWhat it buys
Door (required) Overhead depth camera (Orbbec / RealSense) or an IP camera with a top-down view x86 mini-PC or Jetson Orin Nano, one per store where it stretches In/out counting. The foundation: without it there is no conversion rate.
Frontal (optional) IP camera at face height, beside the entrance Shares the door mini-PC Demographics, staff exclusion, cross-door re-identification.
Zone (optional) IP camera covering the whole zone A modest GPU from 3–4 zones up Dwell, engagement, lost sales, per-display attention.
Checkout (optional) IP camera pointed at whoever is paying Shares the zone machine Ticket-to-visit attribution, and customer identification if the store enables it.

Supported without a custom agent: Hikvision (ISAPI people counting), Dahua and IR beam counters. They count, but they give no face — and without a face there is no staff exclusion, no visits and no purchase units. Fine to start cheap and migrate later.

3. Mounting the cameras

The door

  1. Overhead, not angled. Directly above the doorway, looking down. An angled camera double-counts people walking in single file.
  2. Height 2.6–3.2 m. Lower crops tall people; higher loses depth resolution.
  3. The virtual line goes in the doorway, not two metres inside. This is the single most expensive mistake: a line across a waiting area reports the same person entering six times.
  4. Mind the backlight. A glass front at midday saturates the sensor. If you cannot move the camera, use depth rather than RGB.

Frontal, zone and checkout

  • Frontal: 1.6–1.8 m, slightly above eye line, facing whoever enters. Never against a window.
  • Zone: high and cornered, covering the whole zone with as little overlap into the next as possible.
  • Checkout: framed on the customer, not the cashier. If the cashier is in frame, their staff signature stops being optional.

Signage. In many jurisdictions a visible notice at the entrance is a legal requirement, and everywhere it is the right thing to do. Analitix stores no images, but it does perform biometric processing on the store's own machine: agree it with the customer in writing before switching anything on, and record it on the store form.

4. Odoo setup and per-device keys

  1. Create the store. Analitix → Configuration → New Store Setup. The wizard creates store, doors and devices in one pass. Set the right time zone: hourly buckets and the rota both depend on it.
  2. One key per device. On the device form, Issue key. Format alx_<43 characters>.

    It is shown once. Odoo keeps only a SHA-256 digest, so a lost key cannot be recovered — rotate instead. Never share a key across cameras: a shared key cannot be revoked for one without killing every other.

  3. Mark what is critical. A critical device is one whose silence makes the numbers wrong. Door counters and the checkout camera are; a spare unit is not, and clearing the flag keeps it from paging the technician at 3 a.m.
  4. Edge configuration from Odoo. The device's Edge Configuration field is free-form JSON — line coordinates, camera index, zone polygons. The agent picks it up on its hourly refresh, so a camera is retuned without going back to the store.

5. Installing the edge agent

The agent lives in the repository's edge/ folder, outside the package published to apps.odoo.com.

# On the store's mini-PC
sudo mkdir -p /etc/analitix /var/lib/analitix
sudo cp analitix_agent.py /usr/local/bin/
sudo cp config.example.yaml /etc/analitix/agent.yaml
sudo pip3 install -r requirements.txt

sudo nano /etc/analitix/agent.yaml     # odoo_url + api_key
sudo cp analitix-agent.service /etc/systemd/system/
sudo systemctl enable --now analitix-agent
journalctl -u analitix-agent -f

There are only two required values: odoo_url (https, no exceptions) and api_key. Everything else has a working default.

The settings that matter

KeyDefaultWhen to touch it
sourcelinedemo generates synthetic crossings with no camera: use it to prove the link to Odoo works before the hardware is mounted.
line—Pixel coordinates of the doorway. Better pushed down from Odoo.
send_embeddingsfalseFalse means face vectors never leave the store: the agent pulls staff signatures and compares in memory. Set true only if the hardware cannot hold the signature set.
state_dir/var/lib/analitixHolds the encrypted queue. It must survive a reboot: it is the only thing between a two-hour outage and two hours of lost visitors.
verify_tlstrueSet false only on a lab bench with a self-signed certificate.

No image or video is transmitted or stored, in any mode. The frame is discarded in memory the moment the detection is done.

6. Calibrating the virtual line

  1. Start with source: demo and confirm crossings arrive under Analitix → Operations → Crossing Events.
  2. Switch to source: line and adjust line until it spans the doorway.
  3. Hand-count for fifteen minutes at a busy hour and compare against the dashboard. It is the only method that works. ±5 % is normal; ±30 % means the line is in the wrong place.
  4. Once it is right, push it to the device's edge configuration in Odoo so it survives a reinstall of the agent.

7. Zones, displays and the rota

  • Zones: use the names the staff already use, not yours. Tune the thresholds per zone — a fitting room and a counter do not share a number.
  • Displays: record what is physically on each one. Without it, measured attention cannot be crossed against sales, and that is where the value is.
  • The rota: per zone, per weekday, per hour. Without it alerts reach nobody — or they all land on the fallback user, which is the signal that it has not been filled in.
  • Fallback user: always set one. It is what stops an alert for an uncovered zone from being recorded and read by nobody.

8. Zone ↔ Xibo player mapping

Analitix does not talk to screens directly: it delegates to the Xibo connector when it is installed, detected at runtime. Without the connector, signage rules are still recorded but display nothing — and that shows explicitly in the firing log.

  1. In Xibo, group the players by zone (one display group per zone: window, till, façade).
  2. In Odoo, on each zone, fill in the screen group reference. That is the whole mapping.
  3. Create the signage rules on the store form. The target resolves where before what: "where it happened" sends the piece to the screen of the event's zone.
  4. Test with a quiet-hour rule before a walk-out rule: it fires on its own and nobody has to impersonate a customer.

Check the mapping under Operations → Signage Firings. A firing recorded and not shown is almost always a mistyped group reference.

9. Liveness: the detail people forget

Cameras that do not report liveness send zero. Since the watch list requires a liveness floor above zero, in a store whose agents do no liveness the list never matches anybody, silently.

That is the safe way to fail, and it is deliberate. But if the customer bought the watch list, either enable liveness in the agent or lower the floor knowingly and write it down. What is not acceptable is nudging it until matches start appearing.

10. Network, proxy and TLS

  • Ingest is HTTPS only. A plaintext request is answered 403 tls_required.
  • Odoo behind nginx listens on plain HTTP, so the check reads X-Forwarded-Proto. That is only safe with proxy_mode = True and the proxy as the sole route in. A deployment exposing Odoo directly must not set proxy_mode.
  • For a lab bench with no certificate there is the system parameter analitix.allow_insecure_ingest = 1. It ships as 0: relaxing it must be a conscious, reversible choice rather than a discovery six months later.
  • Ingest is idempotent by client-generated uuid: a network retry never double-counts a crossing.

11. Handover checklist

  • ☐ Every door created, with "Counts visitors" correct on staff and service doors.
  • ☐ Sales matching mode chosen, and registers selected where relevant.
  • ☐ Store time zone correct.
  • ☐ One key per device, none shared, critical devices flagged.
  • ☐ Fifteen-minute hand count against the dashboard, within ±5 %.
  • ☐ Staff face signatures enrolled and tested.
  • ☐ Zones with their thresholds, displays with their products.
  • ☐ Rota filled in and fallback user configured.
  • ☐ End-to-end alert test on every channel that is switched on.
  • ☐ Heartbeats arriving; Operations → Device Health all green.
  • ☐ Technical contacts recorded for the offline e-mail.
  • ☐ Retention window agreed with the customer and set.
  • ☐ Visible signage at the entrance.
  • ☐ Monthly report recipients set.
  • ☐ If they bought the watch list: the security role assigned to two different people (the double control needs them) and liveness resolved.

12. Troubleshooting

SymptomWhere to look
No events at alljournalctl -u analitix-agent -f. A 403 tls_required is http instead of https; a 401 is a bad or revoked key.
Events arrive but no visitsThe agent is sending with send_embeddings: false and no signatures. No face means counts only, no visits.
Double countingAngled camera, or the virtual line inside the shop rather than in the doorway.
Staff appear as customersMissing signatures, or staff exclusion switched off on the store form.
Device flapping to "degraded"Unstable internet. Raise the store's offline threshold; the agent queues and resends.
Jobs piling upOperations → Background Jobs. Check Odoo's cron is alive.
Everything looks fine and there is no dataIs capture paused? The kill switch stays on until somebody clears it.
