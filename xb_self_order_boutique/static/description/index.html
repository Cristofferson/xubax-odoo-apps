Self-Order Kiosk - Boutique Theme

Odoo's kiosk is made for restaurants. This theme dresses it like your shop: large photos, your colors and fonts, a gallery with video and shop words on the buttons.

Odoo's kiosk

With the Boutique Theme

A product page that sells

  • Mosaic with video: the product's published video plays on top, silent and in a loop, and the extra photos follow two by two.
  • Carousel: the customer swipes through the photos.
  • Main photo: Odoo's own, if you prefer it.
  • Options as compact chips, with the price difference next to each name.
  • The photos and video are the ones your website already shows: nothing new to upload.

Your brand, per kiosk

  • Colors: main color, text, photo background and lines.
  • Fonts: Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Montserrat, Jost and Lato are included (SIL Open Font License), or the device's own.
  • Corners: square or rounded.
  • Columns: 2, 3 or 4 on a portrait tablet, one more on wide screens.
  • Price in the list: the price, From + the lowest price of the options, or no price.
  • Words: "View my selection", "Pay at the counter", "Your selection"... or your own, in every language.

The same products with another setup: green, rounded corners, three columns, Playfair and Lato, carousel.

Shop words, start to end

Installable app

  • The tablet installs the kiosk as an app with your name and icon (or your company logo).
  • It opens full screen, with your color on the status bar.
  • In a plain browser tab, the first touch switches to full screen.

Set it up in one screen

Point of Sale › Settings › Mobile self-order & Kiosk, choose the kiosk and switch on Boutique theme. Every kiosk keeps its own setup; the ones without the theme stay as Odoo made them.

Requirements

Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise · Point of Sale with Self-Order Kiosk. Extra photos need the eCommerce app (product images); without it the gallery uses the main photo and the video.

Works with our other kiosk apps: Self-Order Kiosk Filters, Self-Order Kiosk Quotation and Self-Order Kiosk - USB Printer (RawBT). English and Spanish included.
