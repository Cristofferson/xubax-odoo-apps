Showroom Wall

Send any product from your own online store to the showroom screen, in one tap, and let the customer see it full size while you sell.

The problem

A customer and a sales person look at a ring on a tablet. The photo is small, the details are in tiny print, and the big screen on the wall keeps playing the same loop, unrelated to the piece they are talking about.

Setting a signage system to show a given product means leaving the customer and opening another tool.

The fix

Every product of your Odoo online store gets a Show on wall button, visible only to your signed-in staff. One tap and the big screen shows that piece full size, with its video, its name, the metal and size the customer picked, the price (if you want it) and a QR code to take it home.

No new catalog to keep up to date: the wall uses the products, photos, videos and prices you already have in Odoo.

What it does

  • Built for multi-panel walls. On a 3×1 videowall the piece is drawn on the centre panel, so the bezels never cut it; the side panels carry the specs and the QR code. It also works on a single screen.
  • Compare and choose. Two to six pieces side by side, with what sets them apart and, when your products have metal variants, the price of each one. One tap takes a single piece off.
  • Says what the customer picked. The metal and size chosen on the product page travel to the wall, even when that version has no variant yet.
  • Your own words. Each screen has its own tagline and footnote for the comparison (for example «Handmade · Free engraving»), its colours and its language.
  • One wall per branch. Each screen lists the sales people who may drive it, and every tablet remembers its own wall.
  • Price visibility is a setting: hidden, a «from» figure, or the full price.
  • Idle mode. After a few quiet minutes the wall goes back to a shop window of your products, or to whatever it normally shows.
  • Never stays frozen. If the screen's browser stops refreshing, the page reloads itself as soon as the server answers.
  • Optional voice. The wall can say the name of the piece just sent, with any text-to-speech command you configure on your server.

Works on any screen

The wall is a plain web page that polls Odoo. Open it in a smart TV, a browser in kiosk mode or any digital-signage web widget. No signage server is required.

Add-ons, free:

  • Showroom Wall — Self-service Kiosk: customers send a piece to the wall from Odoo's self-service kiosk.
  • Showroom Wall — Xibo: publish the wall on a Xibo screen in one click.

Setup

  1. Create a screen in Website ▸ Configuration ▸ Showroom Screens and choose who may drive it.
  2. Open its wall link on the big screen.
  3. Browse your online store signed in, and tap Show on wall on any product.

Requires Odoo 19 with eCommerce (website_sale). Community and Enterprise. Interface in English and Spanish.
