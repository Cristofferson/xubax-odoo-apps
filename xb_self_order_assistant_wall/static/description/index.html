Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller + Showroom Wall

"Look at the big screen!" The kiosk's virtual seller sends products to the showroom videowall and goes with them.

  • She puts the product the customer likes on the videowall, with the chosen options, alone or next to others to compare.
  • Goes to the wall: she appears small on the wall, with subtitles, and speaks from the wall's speakers.
  • Shows on the wall by itself: every product she opens also goes to the big screen (sales-driven sellers).
  • A tap on the wall's NFC base takes the conversation to the customer's phone.
  • Same rules as the kiosk's own "See it on the wall" button.

Free add-on of Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller (AI). It installs by itself when both apps are present.

Requirements

Odoo 19 · Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller (AI) and Showroom Wall with its kiosk link (xb_showroom_wall_self_order). English and Spanish included.
