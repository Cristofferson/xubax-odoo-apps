Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller - Send by WhatsApp

"Send it to my WhatsApp": the customer gets the products the virtual seller showed and the whole conversation.

  • A keypad on the kiosk: the customer types their number.
  • They receive a WhatsApp message with a link to a page with the products (photos, prices) and the conversation.
  • The contact is found or created by the phone number, with a note of the conversation.
  • Optional: an opportunity in CRM for each customer who asks for it.
  • One click creates the WhatsApp template and sends it to Meta for approval; the button appears once it is approved.

Free add-on of Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller (AI). It installs by itself when both apps are present.

Requirements

Odoo 19 · Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller (AI) and Odoo Enterprise WhatsApp with a connected account. English and Spanish included.
