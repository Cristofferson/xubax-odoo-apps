Product Pricelist Rules – All Types & Search
============================================

Usage
-----

Open any product (**Sales ▸ Products ▸ Products**) and click the
**Pricelist Rules** smart button at the top of the form. The rules are shown
in an informative list with the pricelist, the computation method, the base,
the discount and the resulting price — much clearer than the standard list
for formula and percentage rules.

To browse or audit the whole catalog of rules, use the new menu
**Sales ▸ Products ▸ Price Rules (search)**. It opens the complete list with
a search view, so you can filter or group by product, pricelist or
computation type — handy when a pricelist holds thousands of per-product
rules.
