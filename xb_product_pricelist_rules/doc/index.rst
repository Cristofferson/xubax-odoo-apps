Product Pricelist Rules – All Types & Search
============================================

Usage
-----

Open any product (**Sales ▸ Products ▸ Products**) and look at the
**Pricelist Rules** smart button at the top of the form:

* The count now includes every pricelist rule applicable to the product or
  its variants, regardless of how the price is computed (Fixed, Percentage,
  Formula).
* Click it to review and edit those rules in an informative list that shows
  the pricelist, the computation method, the base, the margin and the
  resulting price.

To browse or audit the whole catalog of rules, use the new menu
**Sales ▸ Products ▸ Price Rules (search)**. It opens the complete list with
the standard search view, so you can filter or group by product, pricelist,
applied-on scope, dates, etc. — handy when a pricelist holds thousands of
per-product rules.
