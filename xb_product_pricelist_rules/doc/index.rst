Product Pricelist Rules – All Types & Search
============================================

Usage
-----

Open any product (**Sales ▸ Products ▸ Products**) and look at the
**Pricelist Rules** smart button at the top of the form:

* The count now includes every pricelist rule applicable to the product or
  its variants, regardless of how the price is computed (Fixed, Percentage,
  Formula).
* Click it to review and edit those rules in an informative list that shows
  the pricelist, the computation method, the base, the margin and the
  resulting price.

Search products inside a pricelist
----------------------------------

Open a pricelist (**Sales ▸ Products ▸ Pricelists**) and click the
**Search products / rules** button above the *Price Rules* tab. It opens that
pricelist's rules in a full list with a search bar, so you can type a product
name and jump straight to its rule — instead of scrolling a tab that can hold
thousands of entries. New rules created from there are pre-assigned to the
current pricelist.

Browse or audit all rules
-------------------------

To browse or audit the whole catalog of rules, use the new menu
**Sales ▸ Products ▸ Price Rules (search)**. It opens the complete list with a
search view that now includes a **Product** field, so you can filter (type a
product name) or group by product, pricelist, applied-on scope, dates, etc. —
handy when a pricelist holds thousands of per-product rules.
