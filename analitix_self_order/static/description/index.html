Analitix - Self-Order Kiosk

Who stops at your kiosk, how long they stay, whether they use it, and whether they buy. Measured with the kiosk's own camera.

One row per visit

Every time someone stops in front of the kiosk, Analitix receives a visit with:

  • when, and how long they stayed;
  • how long they faced the screen, and whether they touched it;
  • how many people were together;
  • an approximate age band and gender, with its confidence (only reliable readings count);
  • whether it ended in a kiosk order or a quotation in Sales, and the amount.

People passing by far from the kiosk are not counted, and stops shorter than 3 seconds are dropped.

Answers you can act on

  • At what time do people stop, and at what time do they buy?
  • How many look at the kiosk but never touch it?
  • Which age band uses it most, and which one converts?
  • Use (%) and Conversion (%) per hour, day, kiosk or store, in pivot and chart views.

Private by design

  • No image ever leaves the tablet. Each frame is read in memory, in the browser, and dropped.
  • Only numbers reach Odoo: seconds, counts, age band, gender.
  • No mini-PC, no door camera, no extra hardware: the kiosk's front camera is enough.
  • Switch it on per kiosk. Put up a notice that a camera is in use, as your local law requires.

Requirements

Odoo 19 · Analitix (XUBAX) · Point of Sale with Self-Order Kiosk · Sales · a kiosk tablet with a front camera and a recent Chrome.

Face detection runs locally with face-api.js (vladmandic, MIT license), included.
