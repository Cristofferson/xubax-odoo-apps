Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller + Appointments

"Do you want to come on Saturday?" The kiosk's virtual seller offers free times and books the visit.

  • Choose the appointment type she books, in the kiosk settings.
  • She offers the next free times, with the type's staff and capacity.
  • She books with the customer's name and phone, at the kiosk or from the customer's phone.
  • The appointment shows in the seller's report.

Free add-on of Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller (AI). It installs by itself when both apps are present.

Requirements

Odoo 19 · Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller (AI) and Odoo Enterprise Appointments. English and Spanish included.
