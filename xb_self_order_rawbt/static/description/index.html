Self-Order Kiosk - USB Printer (RawBT)

Your kiosk prints on the receipt printer it carries: USB or Bluetooth, no network printer needed.

The problem

Many Android kiosks come with a receipt printer built in, connected by USB. Odoo's Self-Order Kiosk can only print on Epson ePOS network printers, so that printer sits unused and the tickets go to a printer at the counter, far from the customer.

The solution

  • The kiosk sends each ticket, as ESC/POS, to the free RawBT app on the same tablet.
  • RawBT prints it on the printer it is paired with: USB, Bluetooth or network.
  • Everything the kiosk prints goes there: order tickets and, with our quotation app, quotations.
  • Switch it on per kiosk; the other kiosks keep their Epson printer.

Made for real thermal printers

  • Paper width: 80 mm (576 dots) or 58 mm (384 dots).
  • Four ticket formats, because cheap printers differ: image in blocks, image in one piece, lines of 24 dots, or an image drawn by RawBT. Pick the one that prints every line whole.
  • Two sending methods (Android intent or rawbt: link) for the browsers and kiosk apps that block one of them.
  • A test page per kiosk, opened on the tablet, prints a sample ticket with each format so you can choose in a minute.
  • Tip: in RawBT, turn off the print dialog and the ticket comes out at once.

How to set it up

  1. Install RawBT from Google Play on the kiosk tablet and print its own test on your printer.
  2. In Odoo: Point of Sale › Settings › Mobile self-order & Kiosk, choose the kiosk and switch on Kiosk printer through RawBT.
  3. Open the test page link on the tablet, tap each format and keep the one that prints best.

Requirements

Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise · Point of Sale with Self-Order Kiosk · an Android kiosk tablet with the RawBT app (by 402d, sold separately in Google Play).
