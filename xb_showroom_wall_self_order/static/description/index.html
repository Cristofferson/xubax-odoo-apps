Showroom Wall — Self-service Kiosk

Customers browsing on their own send a piece from the kiosk to the showroom wall, and compare it with others, without waiting for a sales person.

What it adds

  • A Show on the wall button on the product page of Odoo's self-service kiosk.
  • Customers can put several pieces on the wall and compare them.
  • Pick the wall each kiosk sends to in the Point of Sale settings.

Kept under control

  • Only products ticked Show on the showroom wall get the button, and the server checks it again on every send.
  • Kiosk mode only: the QR menu on a customer's own phone never drives the wall.
  • A short pause between sends keeps a customer from taking the wall over; a sales person's comparison starts over instead of absorbing what a customer sent.

Free add-on. Requires Showroom Wall and Point of Sale self-ordering (pos_self_order).
