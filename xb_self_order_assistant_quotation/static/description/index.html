Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller + Kiosk Quotation

"Print me a quotation": the virtual seller opens the kiosk quotation for what is in the cart.

  • She adds the products to the cart and opens the kiosk's quotation window.
  • The customer leaves their name and phone, and takes the quotation printed at the kiosk or on WhatsApp.
  • Quotations made at the kiosk count in her report as the sale the conversation ended in.

Free add-on of Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller (AI). It installs by itself when both apps are present.

Requirements

Odoo 19 · Self-Order Kiosk - Virtual Seller (AI) and Self-Order Kiosk Quotation. English and Spanish included.
