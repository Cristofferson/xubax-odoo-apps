Self-Order Kiosk Quotation - RawBT

Free add-on: the printed kiosk quotation comes out of the kiosk's own printer.

  • Connects Self-Order Kiosk - Quotation with Self-Order Kiosk - USB Printer (RawBT).
  • When the kiosk prints through RawBT, the customer reads "Your ticket comes out of the kiosk printer" instead of being sent to the counter printer.
  • English and Spanish included.

Requirements

Self-Order Kiosk - Quotation and Self-Order Kiosk - USB Printer (RawBT) (XUBAX). Installs by itself when both are present.
